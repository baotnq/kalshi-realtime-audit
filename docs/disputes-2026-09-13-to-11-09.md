# Kalshi disputed markets, 13 September – 9 November 2026

Every Kalshi market seen with API status `disputed` between 2026-09-13 and 2026-11-09, how each one moved, and how it settled. The section numbers below follow the order of the questions.

**Summary:** 5 events and 48 markets were disputed. No result changed. All 48 were `finalized` when re-read on 2026-11-10. Three of the five events are Mentions contracts.

## Method and limits

- **Source of the dispute set.** `poll.py` reads every closed, not-yet-settled market from Kalshi's public REST API every 5 minutes. A market counts as disputed if it is ever seen with `status = disputed`. Kalshi keeps no status history, so this record starts on 2026-09-13.
- **Polling gap.** Coverage before 2026-09-23 17:10 UTC is partial. Polling has been continuous since then. A dispute that opens and closes between two sweeps is not seen; NBATOTAL below was visible in a single sweep.
- **Other inputs.**
  - Market fields, rules, and the last trade at or before `close_time`: read from `GET /events/{event}?with_nested_markets=true` and `GET /markets/trades` on 2026-11-10.
  - Denominators: every settled market from the full crawl (see `build.py`), settlements to 2026-11-09 12:00 UTC.
- **Times.** All times are UTC. Status times are poll sweep times, so they are accurate to about 5 minutes. `settlement_ts` comes from Kalshi.
- **Volume.** Volume is in contracts.

## 2. Why a dispute hits only some markets of an event

### NBA total points, Minnesota–Milwaukee, 5 Oct 2026: 4 of 25 markets

- **The rules are not the cause.** All 25 markets share one rules text; only the number changes: *"If over X total points are scored in the Minnesota vs Milwaukee Pro Basketball game originally scheduled for Oct 5, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes."*
- **The real total was 213.** The final was Timberwolves 116, Bucks 97 in the preseason ([Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nba/minnesota-timberwolves-milwaukee-bucks-2026100515/)). Kalshi's `expiration_value` is also 213.
- **The disputed strikes are the ones nearest the final total.** The four were Over 211.5 (yes), Over 213.5 (no), Over 215.5 (no) and Over 217.5 (no). Over 209.5 was not disputed. The other 21 settled at 03:05, eight minutes before the four.
- **The busiest market was among them.** Over 213.5, which missed by half a point, was the busiest market in the event (149,314 contracts, last trade 0.08).

### NBA 3rd-quarter spread, Memphis–Sacramento, 25 Oct 2026: 13 of 13 markets

- **Sacramento won the third quarter by 2.** Sacramento scored 31 and Memphis 29 ([ESPN box score](https://www.espn.com/nba/boxscore/_/gameId/401909867); final 123–112 Memphis).
- **That margin falls between the two lowest strikes.** "Sacramento by over 1.5" settled yes and "by over 2.5" settled no. The whole event was disputed for about 20 minutes and settled unchanged.

### Melania, 15 Sep: 5 of 12 markets

The five disputed markets are exactly the five that settled **no**: Donald, FEMA, Peace, Scholarship, and *Event does not qualify*. None of the seven **yes** markets was disputed.

### Trump, 28 Sep: 1 of 30 markets

- **Only one word was disputed.** "Crypto / Bitcoin" (60,713 contracts) was the only disputed market. The event had 17 other **no** markets and 12 **yes** markets.
- **The market closed early and then reopened.** It closed early at 18:35, which is the behaviour defined for "the event occurs", and was disputed. At 18:42 it was determined **no**. It then left the closed set and traded again: trades continue between 18:47 and 20:57, including 10,295 contracts in 19:30–19:40. It closed with the rest of the event at 20:57 and settled **no** at 21:28.
- **The likely reading is not confirmed by Kalshi.** The early close was triggered as if the word had been said, the dispute found it had not, and the market was reopened.

### Bernie, Amarillo, 19 Oct: 25 of 25 markets

The whole event was disputed. *Event does not qualify* settled **yes** and all 24 word markets settled **no**. That market last traded at 0.80 before close, so the market had already priced non-qualification. The reason the rally did not qualify is not established:

## 5. What "dispute" means in Kalshi's rulebook

Source: [KalshiEX LLC Rulebook, version 1.15, 17 January 2025](https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/KalshiEX_LLC_Rulebook.pdf). Whether a later version exists was not checked.

- **The rulebook never calls a market outcome "disputed".** The word appears only for arbitration between participants (Chapter 10) and for compliance matters. The API statuses `disputed` and `amended` have no rulebook definition.
- **Rule 7.1, the Market Outcome Review Process, is the closest match.**
  - (a): *"Before Settlement, Kalshi may initiate the Market Outcome Review Process, at its sole discretion … the Outcome Review Committee will determine the final Market Outcome … If the Market Outcome Review Process is initiated, Kalshi will post on its website that the Contract's Market Outcome is under review."*
  - (b): *"… determine a final Market Outcome within a 24 hour period after the Outcome Review Process is initiated."*
  - (c): *"The determinations made by the Outcome Review Committee are final."*
- **Rule 6.3(d):** *"Before Settlement, Kalshi may, at its sole discretion initiate the Market Outcome Review Process as provided in Rule 7.1."*
- **Rule 6.3(b):** if the outcome cannot be determined, Kalshi may pay out at the last traded price, or the Outcome Review Committee makes *"a binding determination of fair allocation"*. None of the 48 markets settled at a value other than 0 or 1.

**Observed against Rule 7.1(b).** All five reviews ended well within 24 hours; the longest was about 6 h 16 min.

**What the API cannot tell.** The API does not show whether a `disputed` status is a Rule 7.1 review started by Kalshi, a challenge from a member, or an internal check. This page does not look for the "under review" notices on Kalshi's website.
